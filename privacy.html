Privacy Policy

youtube-upload-app is a personal tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

The app requests only the youtube.upload scope. It uses this permission solely to upload video files chosen by the owner to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any user data with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
